## 1. Summary

The revised manuscript reports a dose-response audit of refusal-direction ablation on Qwen3-4B-Instruct, designed to price the "introspection unlock" of Macar et al. (2026), and instead documents two manipulation-check failures: the standard direction-selection procedure returned a direction with zero held-out bypass power (refusal 0.97 at every λ, 0/100 prompt flips), and the pilot-selected injection strength destroyed the free-text report channel (0% parse under injection vs 100% clean), leaving the standard metric family undefined. The surviving 0.72 forced-choice score is flat in the dose and is explained by concept-bank geometry (0.96 isolated vs 0.43 clique, replicated across two scoring protocols), which the paper reads as vector-to-logit leakage rather than introspection. The contribution is reframed as a six-item positive-control checklist for concept-injection studies.
