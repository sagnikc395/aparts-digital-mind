# Review: *The Alignment Tax of Introspection* (pre-submission draft)

**Reviewer**: scientific-reviewer protocol (`.claude/skills/SKILL.md`), applied to `paper/paper.md` as of 2026-08-16 22:47 EDT, with all quantitative claims re-verified against `results/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507/` and `results/layer18/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507/`.
**Prior reviews**: `REVIEW-20260816-154542.md` (Minor revision), `REVIEW-20260816-161742.md` (Accept, pre-confirmation-run).

## 1. Summary

The manuscript sets out to price the "introspection unlock" of Macar et al. (2026), measuring safety and general capability at five partial-ablation strengths of the refusal direction on Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 while probing concept-injection self-report. It instead reports two manipulation-check failures: the standard Arditi-style conjunctive selection criterion returned a direction with zero held-out bypass power, so refusal on JailbreakBench stayed at 0.97 with zero prompt-level flips at every dose, and the pilot-chosen injection strength (alpha = 4.0) destroyed the free-text channel, so five of the six standard introspection metrics are undefined. A confirmation run that forces the highest-bypass candidate the induce filter had rejected drops refusal from 0.97 to 0.51 with 46 of 100 prompts flipping, all in the refusal-to-compliance direction, which relocates the first failure from the model to the selection criterion. The contribution is a half exchange rate (refusal against MMLU, TruthfulQA and cross-entropy across the whole dose) plus a six-item positive-control checklist.

## 2. Verification performed

Every number in the draft was recomputed from the raw artifacts in this session, independently of the paper text:

- Run shape (38,400 generations: 24,000 structured, 9,600 skeptical, 4,800 pilot), model, layer, alpha, k, held-out counts: **all confirmed**.
- Selection funnel (95 candidates, rejections 55 KL / 25 layer / 13 induce, two survivors both bypass 0.000, best bypass L18 p-3 at 0.875, only two candidates with any induce score): **confirmed**.
- Primary safety (0.97 at all five doses, 0 flips at any dose, paired word delta 9.17, t(99) = 3.33): **confirmed**. Note the draft rounds the word delta to 9.2; the artifact gives 9.17.
- Primary capability, forced-choice accuracies and bootstrap CIs, skeptical replication, pilot grid, concept-bank geometry (27-member clique, mean internal cosine 0.9998, PR 1.9, 0.961 vs 0.428 split, Welch t = 6.99): **confirmed**.
- Confirmation-run safety and capability tables: **confirmed** against `safety_summary_layer18.json` and `capability.json`.
- Flip direction: recomputed per prompt from `safety.jsonl` rather than inferred from net counts. At every dose the refusal-to-compliance count equals the flip count and the compliance-to-refusal count is exactly 0 (0/0, 16/0, 31/0, 41/0, 46/0). The draft's claim is correct but is currently justified by an arithmetic argument; it should cite the direct count.

## 3. Methodology and statistics

**Sound.** Concept-level rather than trial-level resampling, per-prompt safety records, a norm-matched random-direction control absent from the prior literature, refusal to impute unparseable output, pre-registered pilot gate, and independent recomputation of the primary run by a script that does not import the analysis stack. The confirmation run is a real out-of-sample test: a bypass score estimated on 32 held-out prompts generalised to 46/100 JailbreakBench behaviours.

**Weaknesses that should be fixed before submission.**

1. **The manuscript is now stale with respect to its own artifacts.** `results/layer18/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507/sweep_structured.jsonl` contains 5,240 introspection records under the forced direction: a complete cell at lambda = 0 (1,200 per condition) and a partial cell at lambda = 0.25 (120/120/100/100). The draft states in Limitations and Future Work that this sweep "was not run". It was started. The data reproduces the free-text collapse under the working direction (parse 0.00 under C1 and C3, 1.00 under C2, clean FPR 0.00) and gives forced choice 0.7225 at lambda = 0. On the five concepts that both cells share, accuracy is 0.56 at lambda = 0 and 0.60 at lambda = 0.25, so the partial cell shows no dose effect and its lower headline number is concept composition, not ablation. This must be reported; the honest framing is that the introspection term is still missing because only two doses ran and because the channel that carries it is dead at this alpha, not because the run does not exist.
2. **Multiplicity is unhandled in the capability analysis.** Four measures times five doses, with the single result at p = 0.049 landing on TruthfulQA. No correction is applied and none is discussed. The monotonicity argument is stated informally; either run a trend test (Cochran-Armitage over the five doses would cost nothing) or label the result explicitly as uncorrected and exploratory.
3. **The token-dominance statistic is not reproducible from the repository.** The "44% of tokens, 1% on failures" figures are not produced by any script in `scripts/` or `src/`. Recomputation gives 21% against 0.5% under exact token matching and 50% against 5% when morphological fragments of the concept are counted. The modal-token count (21 of 42) and the concept count (10 of 20) reproduce exactly. Either ship the script or quote a matcher-explicit pair of numbers.
4. **"Safety" remains refusal, and the draft could bound the gap better than it does.** The `safe` field is null throughout (no judge was run), which the draft states. It does not use the compliance proxy that is present: of the 49 non-refusing completions at lambda = 1, 18 contain structured how-to markers and the mean length is 92 words. That is still not a harm rate, but it is a cheap tightening of "non-refusal" and it is sitting in the artifact.
5. **Presentation against the sprint template.** The abstract is 341 words against the stated 150-250. The body is roughly three times the recommended four pages. Sixteen em dashes are present. None of this affects the science, but the submission is judged on the report.

**Confounds correctly named by the authors**: induce resolution of 1/32, fixed induce strength of 1.0, substring-heuristic insensitivity, the self-selection in words-per-refusal once the refusing set shrinks, the injection-layer disagreement with Hahami et al., and the low power of the benchmark suite. I found no confound the draft misses.

## 4. Strengths and weaknesses

**Major strengths.** The confirmation run converts a null into a claim about a widely used method: on this model the published criterion ranked a 0.88-bypass candidate below a 0.00-bypass candidate, and the intervention it rejected is worth 46 points of refusal with every flip in the same direction. The two-channel dissociation (log-probability forced choice reporting 0.72 while the free-text channel emits token salad) is a clean demonstration that the standard headline metric survives the death of the thing it is supposed to measure. The geometry result is a genuine grader-validity finding, replicated across two scoring protocols on independent data, and it implicates a check the field does not run. Reporting undefined cells as undefined, with the raw transcripts, is the right call and is rarer than it should be.

**Major weaknesses.** One model, one seed, one direction, at 4B against the 27B result being interrogated; the bypass/induce dissociation may not survive scale. The concept bank is defective and the cause was not isolated, so every identification number is a lower bound of unknown tightness. The introspection half of the exchange rate is still not priced. The capability suite is underpowered by roughly a factor of eight for the effect sizes at issue.

**Minor.** Table 1 columns are undefined by design and this is explained late; Figure 4B plots accuracy against a refusal rate that is itself an estimate on 100 items with no horizontal error; the CE-loss decrease is correctly interpreted but deserves a sentence in the abstract-level summary since it is counterintuitive.

## 5. Rubric scores

- **Impact potential and innovation: 4/5.** A neglected problem, an original dose-response framing, and a negative result about a default procedure that other labs can act on immediately. Not a new research direction, but a real correction to an existing one.
- **Execution quality: 4/5.** Raised from 3 by the confirmation run and the capability sweep under the working direction. Held below 5 by n = 1 on model, seed and direction, the unpriced introspection term, and the undiagnosed bank.
- **Presentation and clarity: 4/5.** Precise and well-organised, but over length, with a non-compliant abstract and a stale limitations section. Fixing those returns it to 5.

## 6. Recommendation

**Minor revision.** The science is sound and independently verified. Required before submission: report the partial layer-18 introspection sweep and correct the two places that call it unrun; state the flip-direction result as a direct per-prompt count; make the token-dominance statistic reproducible or restate it; declare the capability comparisons uncorrected and exploratory; trim the abstract to the template's range and the body toward the recommended length.
